Assessment summary

Likelihood is conditional on the rainfall shown and the modeled post-fire conditions. It is not the annual chance of a debris flow and does not predict where debris will travel. This is not an official warning or evacuation threshold.

Assessment provenance and interpretation

Separate rainfall windows are separate estimates: do not sum, average or combine their probabilities. M1 and M3 are distinct models, not an ensemble or uncertainty bounds. Input coverage is usable spatial support, not confidence.

The published study-area range is 0.2–8 km². Preprocessing equivalence to published assessments remains unresolved. These results do not estimate debris-flow volume, runout, inundation or damage, and are not an official USGS assessment.

Short rainfall PDS recurrence intervals are not interchangeable with annual exceedance probabilities. NOAA, when used, supplies design rainfall, not the event catalog.

Displayed-row CSV: current page and displayed rainfall units; numeric values retain full precision. Probability columns are explicitly labeled fractions (0–1), not percentages. Context columns identify the assessment, filters, page and units.
