Estimate debris-flow likelihood for recently burned watersheds in the Western United States. Staley et al. (2017) recommend M1; it performed most consistently against regional rainfall thresholds in their test dataset. M3 provides an alternative when dNBR is unavailable and requires soil thickness and basin ruggedness.

InputM1M3
TerrainFraction of watershed both at least 23° slope and moderately/highly burnedBasin ruggedness: vertical relief divided by square root of watershed area
FireMean differenced Normalized Burn Ratio (dNBR)Fraction of watershed moderately/highly burned
SoilMean soil erodibility (K), prepared through RUSLEMean soil thickness, using SSURGO first and STATSGO as fallback
RainfallRainfall over 15, 30 or 60 minutesRainfall over 15, 30 or 60 minutes
