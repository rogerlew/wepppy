WEPPcloud

Water Erosion Prediction Project

Data-driven watershed hydrology and erosion modeling to support decision-making.

WEPPcloud is an online interface for running WEPP (Water Erosion Prediction Project) Model. Access tools, explore documentation, or review the latest watershed analytics.

What is WEPPcloud?

WEPPcloud is an open-source, online watershed modeling platform that automates the acquisition and processing of geospatial resources and performs process-based modeling to predict runoff, erosion, and sediment delivery in minutes. Users can select from a variety of processing options for climate inputs, wildfire, treatments, and land-management scenarios to support side-by-side comparisons and model calibration. They can then analyze and view results through interface reports and maps, and export results for planning, research, and post-fire response.

Attributions and data sources

WEPPcloud leverages open data, research-quality datasets, and mapping services from partners worldwide.

Map services

Project maps use Google Terrain and Satellite. The Run Atlas uses OpenStreetMap contributors and CARTO tiles.

Optional Forest Service Basemap for project maps. USDA, U.S. Forest Service · Forest Service - Geospatial Office. Google Terrain is the default.

Hydrography flowlines come from the U.S. Geological Survey NHDPlus HR network flowlines. View the flowline metadata.
