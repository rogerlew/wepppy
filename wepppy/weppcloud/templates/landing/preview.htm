Watershed hydrology and erosion modeling

WEPPcloud is an open-source platform for the Water Erosion Prediction Project (WEPP) model. Predict runoff, erosion, and sediment delivery for watershed planning, research, and post-fire response.

Explore interfaces Read the user guide

About WEPPcloud

WEPPcloud assembles and processes geospatial data for watershed modeling. Compare climate inputs, wildfire conditions, treatments, and land-management scenarios side by side. Use the reports and maps to review results and calibrate the model, or export results for further analysis.
