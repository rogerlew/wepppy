Predict watershed runoff and erosion

WEPPcloud is an open-source platform for the Water Erosion Prediction Project (WEPP) model. Predict runoff, erosion, and sediment delivery for watershed planning, research, and post-fire response.

About WEPPcloud

Assemble inputs

Build watershed models from terrain, soil, vegetation, and climate data.

Compare scenarios

Compare the effects of wildfire, treatments, and land management side by side.

Review and export

Use maps and reports to calibrate your model or export results for further analysis.
