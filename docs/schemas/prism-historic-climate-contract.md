## Spatial semantics and model transformations

Single retrieves the native cell containing the watershed centroid. Multiple builds that same centroid climate then applies existing monthly PRISM precipitation ratios and temperature offsets to each hillslope. All hillslopes inherit its PRN-quantized wet/dry calendar (subject to final output rounding). MultipleInterpolated retrieves nearest native cells for watershed and hillslope centroids, deduplicates them, and applies no monthly PRISM revision. Hillslopes sharing a cell and generation settings receive identical daily climate weather values. Native cell centers, existing selected CLIGEN station and common saved seed govern per-cell generation; centroid/hillslope names are mappings, not random seed inputs.

Use the bulk cache contract in ADR-0081. PRISM supplies ppt (mm), tmin/tmax/tdmean (C), soltotal (MJ/m2/day). Preserve source values. A working copy supplies CLIGEN observed precipitation/temperatures through the established PRN writer and observed quality guard. Preserve the existing PRN quantization: precipitation rounds to 0.01 inch (0.254 mm), temperatures to integer Fahrenheit (5/9 C). Trace precipitation may therefore round to zero; record its dates/count and verify wet/dry calendars against PRN-quantized forcing, not unquantized source. Do not restore trace precipitation onto a zero-duration generated storm. Replace CLI radiation with soltotal * (1e6 / 41840) langleys/day; preserve PRISM daily date labels (24 hours ending 12:00 UTC). Join GridMET wind by date at the watershed centroid, sharing wind among hillslopes as in Daymet; preserve existing speed/direction conversion conventions. This is daily label alignment, not an exact subdaily shift. Wind acquisition/coverage errors fail explicitly. CLIGEN supplies unobserved within-day storm characteristics using the selected station and existing adjust_mx_pt5/seed settings; spatial rain occurrence is observed only at daily cell scale.

Apply the existing observed-forcing dewpoint floor to model inputs: max(raw tdmean, final quantized Tmin). After monthly hillslope revision, recompute from raw source tdmean and revised Tmin; do not reuse centroid-clipped dewpoint on cooler hillslopes. Never alter raw cached/project source dewpoint. Record changed-day counts and before/after values in derived diagnostics. No clipping UI option or change to other datasets. Existing precipitation scaling still runs after generation under its unchanged contract.
