## Candidate decision

The initial A03 candidate used a DOUBLE sum and a downward REAL cast. It fixes
the observed history but fails constructed mixed-exponent and cancellation controls;
reject that algorithm for general admission. A04 instead carries the primitive
REAL supplies into an error-free binary64 expansion before comparing capacities
or computing remaining room. Preserve canopy-first/residue-second branch priority.
Certify accepted stores against the exact represented budget and certify maximal
REAL acceptance using its successor. Any canopy-limited representational remainder
continues as throughfall; it is not reassigned to residue. This uses no empirical
epsilon or minimum storage floor.

Strict operation ordering is required for the original expansion helper. Compiler
reassociation/unsafe math is disabled for its object. The finite binary32 primitive
range fits the binary64 expansion without overflow or underflow. Exact internal
allocation does not make every downstream REAL flux exact: returned binary64 and
persisted REAL remainders retain their explicit rounding boundaries.

Reconstruct signed remaining supply using the same budget-minus-accepted-stores
order. Preserve every representable residual as throughfall; do not discard it or
spend dry-reference soil water. IDAT's net interception and storm depletion,
both daily/hourly water-balance net-input calculations and ROCHEK's interception
subtraction must be assessed together. Preserve ROCHEK's effective-plane p and
geometry semantics; do not replace them with local rainfall/melt primitives.
Furrow irrigation remains excluded from interception and added at its existing
consumer. Signed downstream requests and their unmet-withdrawal guard remain.

## Alternatives and limits

Reject a FIN floor, arbitrary tolerance or removal of the storage guard. A residue-
only expression in current-input coordinates fixes the observed example but does
not resolve canopy-limited round-up or re-associated consumer subtraction. Global
REAL-kind changes introduce unrelated numerical effects. Limiting changes to a
shared original scalar allocation/remainder helper and confirmed consumers makes
the behavior testable without changing storage formats or scientific coefficients.

Standalone controls are necessary but insufficient. Require actual native replay,
complete calendars, producer/consumer readback, wider failure-class controls and
non-target regressions. Negative relative coordinates elsewhere and frost-policy
compatibility remain separate scientific questions. No general retention, surface
armor, frozen-water or physical-closure admission follows automatically.
