and executable hashes to the public receipt, and matched stdout to reported
observations. Reconstructed initialization and overflow arithmetic independently
with explicit float32 operations, without importing the campaign generator.

from a successful capacity candidate. The native denominator control is valid;

source change. FIN is only a branch selector in the extracted overflow blocks.
No complete-routine execution, frost conservation or annual recovery is implied.
The downstream-expression prerequisite added to the canonical rock contract is
supported by this counterexample and by the independently audited consumer map.
