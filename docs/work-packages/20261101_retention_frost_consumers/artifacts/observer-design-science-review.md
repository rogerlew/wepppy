## Actual callsites

The two lower-layer sites are instrumented only for a non-bottom current layer, matching the following executed ELSEIF ST/UL division. Their recorded layer identifies the lower layer whose operands are consumed, not the outer current layer. LAT observations immediately precede the qualifying lateral quotient after the native branch conditions. HK positive-log and fallback-guard paths use separate slots. No logged local is newly read from an unsupported branch in these additions.

The logger receives native-rounded quotient operands; the lateral differences are computed in native precision before conversion to double. Slots 2–4 test positive denominators without treating signed ST numerator values as automatically invalid physical water. Slot 1 checks the constitutively admissible positive-conductivity log ratio 0<ratio<1, a stronger condition than merely avoiding a zero log. Slot 5 counts guard execution, not an executed log/division or physical admissibility pass.

## Required interpretation

Daily summaries distinguish sites, OFEs and layers and retain counts, numerator/denominator extrema, minimum margin, nonfinite/domain failure counts and positive frozen-thickness exposure. The HK ratio/margin is dimensionless; lower-layer and lateral numerator/denominator quantities are meters. The logger's frozen count is exposure to positive coarse FROZEN, not a validation of frozen geometry, absolute ice or fine-grid support.

The bad counter resets daily. B records therefore retain the first invalid payload per day/site/OFE/layer, not solely the first invalid event in the full history. They do not retain the within-day hour. Label the scope correctly and do not invent exact incident timing beyond these records. Nonfinite payloads must remain explicit invalid-domain evidence; summary extrema alone cannot replace the first-bad payload.

Selected RD_LAT detail contains ST-DRFC and UL-DRFC rather than separate ST, UL and DRFC. This is sufficient to inspect the executed division domain; it cannot alone reconstruct each constituent or prove phase/flux ownership. Existing stage snapshots and accepted-flux observations must support any such stronger claim. Full-history daily domain summaries do not establish full-year stock/flux closure.

The daily record count is the number of dates with recorded calls, including initialization when applicable, not necessarily the full weather calendar. Require footer/count reconciliation, valid declared support and selected-detail/summary agreement in the reader. Missing branch execution remains a coverage gap. Ordinary-output and full FH byte parity remain prerequisites for treating the instrumentation as observation-only evidence.
