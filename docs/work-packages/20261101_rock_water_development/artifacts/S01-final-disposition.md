## Actual ownership finding

Four complete 2000–2015 histories compared default and optional retention, each
plain and observed. Seven ordinary output files match exactly within each pair.
Independent readback joins initialized coefficients and actual consumers by their
binary32 bits. Both policies have 15 dynamic FC observations and 15 actually
executed unsaturated HK power observations; 10 slots on OFEs 1/2 use OFE 3's
coefficients instead of their own. Initialization-only calls were distinguished
from dynamic calls; entering PERC was not substituted for executing its power.

Maximum absolute own-versus-consumed FC capacity differences are 21.636650 mm
(default) and 17.755670 mm (optional); HK exponent differences are 1.880088 and
1.737858. These are coefficient differences, not hydrograph errors. No repaired
model was run, so correction efficacy, affected-project counts and corrected
runoff/storage/ET magnitudes remain unmeasured. Exact observer parity establishes
observation neutrality; finite domains do not make ownership correct.

The coefficient declarations, initialization blocks and PURK/PERC consumers are
unchanged across revision 4 source `b01a739957bef5aacf35b4a89bc13eddc9d94804`,
program ancestors and final source `d2e14d96af5942409c9a315dc8594fcc164a702a`.
This establishes source preexistence, not unchanged exposure or harmlessness.
Revision 5's changed supply and phase histories can change consequences.

Prior accepted campaigns retain their actual measured evidence. The observed P03
repeated-material profiles have equal initialized coefficients across OFEs, but
repeated source material alone is not a general safety test: management and layer
support can change initialization. No additional safe-use class is admitted here.

## Recommendation and release boundary

Preserve the default mass-complement preparation and optional-only contract.
Closer retention integrals do not establish calibration, universal cancellation or
default adoption. Do not add a third formula, relax eligibility, rewrite existing
soils, or repair native ownership within this investigation's authority.

Local revision 5 artifacts can document the known defect and bounded validation.
They do not close the correctness finding, waive the package closure gate or
authorize unrestricted heterogeneous use or deployment. A separately authorized
native correction and affected-consumer validation are required to resolve that
gate; documentation alone cannot do so. The durable boundary is recorded in the
[whole-soil contract](../../../schemas/whole-soil-water-capacity-contract.md#known-native-coefficient-ownership-defect)
and [ADR-0132](../../../adrs/ADR-0132-optional-ssurgo-retention-builder.md#risk-rejection-and-rollback).

policy companions and matched soil/hillslope/PASS/watershed provenance. The same
binary filename does not identify its bytes. This compatibility rule is not a
regeneration instruction or a safety admission. Defaults remain unchanged, no
existing project soils or persisted outputs were rewritten, and no deployment
occurred in S01.
