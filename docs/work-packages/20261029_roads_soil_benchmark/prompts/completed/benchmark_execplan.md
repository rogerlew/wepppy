## Surprises & Discoveries


WEPPcloud paved outslope selects `3p*1.sol`, fswepp2 selects `3p*2.sol`. For silt,
rill erodibility is 0.0003 versus 0.000001 and critical shear 2 versus 10 Pa.
These are input differences, not measured sediment ratios. WEPPcloud uses
inslope management across designs; fswepp2 selects design-specific management.
Both divide road Ki and Kr by four for low/no traffic.

The generic `to7778` converter estimates missing conductivity with Rosetta and
adds restrictive-layer properties; it is not a neutral format-only conversion.
