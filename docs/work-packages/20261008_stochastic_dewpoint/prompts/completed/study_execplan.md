## Surprises & Discoveries


The previous study tested removing a floor from observed GridMET-derived humidity.
It does not answer whether adding a floor to CLIGEN improves stochastic output.
Retained station files represent Canoga Park, Walla Walla and Wenatchee; their
elevations and historical climate can differ substantially from the hillslopes.

Stochastic output contains a terminal blank line; the initial strict parser
rejected six complete files. Fixed the parser to accept terminal whitespace only,
preserving generated bytes and all initial logs. No climates were regenerated
to avoid quality messages. Twenty-six of 30 completed series report unmet
internal random-deviate quality targets (454 messages); four have none. This is
a generator diagnostic limitation, not a missing-day or WEPP-completion failure.
Retain every declared seed and also analyze the diagnostic-free subset.

The forest stations generate only 22–37% of the previous local GridMET
precipitation, so their ET mismatch cannot be attributed chiefly to humidity.
All four diagnostic-free realizations are at Walla Walla. They retain clipping's
pooled cycle-RMSE and distribution advantage in all 12 applicable comparisons.

## Outcomes & Retrospective


All 180 WEPP cases completed, with exactly paired non-dewpoint forcing and
unchanged model parameters. All 36 pooled comparisons improve with clipping for
cycle RMSE and within-month distribution distance; 35 improve for cycle MAE.
Seed-specific counts are 360/360, 359/360 and 347/360 respectively. Annual ET
falls by 0.04–12.28 mm across the 90 pairs. Effects are modest compared with
station/local climate mismatch. Generator warnings remain explicit, and the
native stochastic default is unchanged. The figure, tables and full raw outputs
are retained. This is a completed screening baseline, not certification of the
generator or evidence that clipped humidity is universally more accurate.
