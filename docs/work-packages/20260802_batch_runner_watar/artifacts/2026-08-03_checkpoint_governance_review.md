# Superseded REM-06 Draft Checkpoint Governance Review

**Reviewer**: Independent governance control agent
**Date**: 2026-08-03 UTC
**Mode**: Read-only
**Verdict**: Blocked; two high and two medium findings

## High Findings

1. The then-named REM-06 draft lacked authority to use the bounded cross-owner remediation exception.
   The standard limits borrowing to a production defect and says it is not a
   general way to start planned domain work early. The decision instead
   classifies a new enhancement and borrows planned SURF-02A/B ownership.
   Reclassify and evidence a canonical defect, or establish an authority path
   that explicitly permits this enhancement and owner borrowing with operator
   authorization and the required standard/register amendment.
2. Explicit approval of the exact normative delta is not evidenced. The
   operator's execute direction predates material choices in the matrix,
   including default-enabled old-state behavior, clone-only resync, generic UI,
   topology, and timestamp ownership. Present and obtain affirmative approval
   of the exact revised contract and governance classification.

## Medium Findings

1. WEPP prerequisites and recovery are not exact. Enumerate TaskEnum
   prerequisites, artifact paths/validity, permitted recovery helpers, and
   failure behavior.
2. Retry/output semantics conflict. Package text selects missing/stale outputs,
   while the decision and matrix make the timestamp authoritative. Choose one
   rule and align all documents and tests.

## Positive Observations

The checkpoint correctly records its starting revision, high security impact,
exclusions, additive compatibility intent, pending conformance, registration
cross-links, and review/ancestor gates.
