# Scientific design review

Disposition: the bounded preparation experiment and fixed six-case history panel are scientifically defensible. This is design acceptance, not approval of a particular native runner/freeze or production promotion. Two closure items are identified below.

## Preparation semantics

The producer implements the proposed strict assumption screen: both final branches must be direct-source; original finite density must lie between zero and 2.65; resolved mass fraction lies in [0,0.85]; original source limits are positive and strictly ordered; their volume inverses are strictly below assumed fine-earth porosity. Only experimental prepared FC/WP change. The assumption screen does not establish native admission or measured porosity. Rounded emitted values, native density, entrapped air and subsequent guards must remain separately observed.

The retained 72-case inventory has 60 paired actual writer outputs and twelve prior exclusions retained without reattempt. Missing density, zero/equal limits, fallback and above-cap records remain unchanged with explicit reasons. Prior exclusions are historical statuses, not newly reproduced failures. Reexecuting Horizon invokes Rosetta even when the final selected branch is direct source; dependency binding is appropriate.

The screen avoids claiming that an uncapped source inverse cancels the native 85% cap. It also avoids manufacturing admissibility through clipping. Its eligibility outcomes depend on the stipulated nonporous additive-volume and density assumptions. They are not quality ratings of the source measurements.

## Paired native panel and interpretation

The fixed six source horizons cover zero rock, diagnostic ratios above and below one, all four source mapunits, low original density and exactly 85% mass. Repetition of a controlled horizon over three OFEs remains an experimental context, not component selection or a reconstructed field profile. Six full baseline/candidate pairs are adequate for this finite initialization and existing-conductivity-observer checkpoint, not broad constitutive validation.

Require zero-rock byte identity for actual generated files and ordinary native outputs. For changed pairs, retained differences are observations, not calibration success. Compare source, prepared, emitted and observed initialization values with their distinct units and rounding. Native guard interventions are outcomes. Evaluate realized conductivity and drainage expression domains using observed operands, while distinguishing initialized-domain reconstruction from dynamically executed branch evidence.

The existing binary's limited observations cannot establish complete drainage or accepted-flux closure. This is an explicit acceptable scope limit; successful complete histories cannot remove it. A future production proposal still needs dynamic-domain and water-ownership evidence.
