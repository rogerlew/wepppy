## Decision

The bounded prototype successfully reaches the native consumer and changes
retention. It is not approved for production. Initial relative stocks change,
but the associated dry-reference shift cancels in absolute initial water and
available UL-ST to native roundoff. This is not a repair of structural capacity.
Frozen-water validity remains untested because the preserved KSFLAG0 configuration
skips frost processing; complete weather histories alone did not establish it.

Next, declare a paired frost-enabled consumer experiment with explicit conductivity
and drainage operand/branch observations and accepted-flux accounting. Any change
to experimental process enablement must be declared and separated from retention.
Do not promote an inverse or global porosity multiplier from this checkpoint.
