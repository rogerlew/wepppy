## Scientific interpretation and admission

The exact-arithmetic domain helper correctly demonstrates a proposed ceiling with FC/UL=2/3 and UL-DRFC=0, plus zero-rock identity and the ratio-one zero logarithm. These are counterexamples to sufficient admission by ordered limits alone, not compiled branch results or evidence of a singularity in every actual soil.

The contract correctly distinguishes mass and volume bases, nonporous additive-volume assumptions, source/prepared/emitted/native density, native caps and guards, dry references, absolute and relative stocks, and overflow/accepted-flux ownership. Density interpretation remains an explicit assumption of the diagnostic; no source-density equivalence is established merely by naming a field. A volume inverse above unity or assumed porosity indicates incompatibility of the combined assumptions and inputs, not necessarily a bad whole-soil measurement.

The findings explicitly resolve the retained metadata naming error: `source_native_density_equal_assumption` means hypothetical native density equals rounded emitted BD, not original source density. Preserve the executed helper and receipt with this erratum.

The next bounded prototype and native consumer investigation are warranted. They must retain incompatible cases and zero/missing/fallback semantics, and resolve conductivity/drainage domains and displaced-water ownership before any production promotion. Current evidence establishes neither a porous-fragment law nor annual/runoff consequences. No scientific blocker remains in the reviewed findings or contract.
