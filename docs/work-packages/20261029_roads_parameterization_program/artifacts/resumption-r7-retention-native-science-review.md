## Decision and justified next boundary

The tested lane has a supported **non-null paired output response** and inadequate
target benefit, with adverse movement in both controls' reference agreement.
Reject it as a general rescue candidate here while retaining the experimental
builder and provenance evidence as diagnostic work. This is not proof that the
source retention inverse is universally wrong, nor evidence to admit it as a
physical default.

Close the bounded trial after reconciling the separately reviewed initialization
inventory/mapping. No broader panel is warranted simply to seek a favorable
subset. If causal research continues, first use these retained histories to
declare a specific unresolved mechanism and its discriminating observable.
The mixed OFE response warrants distinguishing sediment supply/routing from
retention-driven water partition; a dynamic drainage claim would require actual
PERC/branch operands, not an estimate from initialized ratios. Any such new
instrumentation or study needs its own bounded declaration, controls and parity
gate. It does not authorize parameter fitting, threshold changes, another
multiplier or withheld-outcome inspection.
