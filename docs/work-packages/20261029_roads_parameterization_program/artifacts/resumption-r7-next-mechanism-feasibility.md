**Assessment: no additional parameter trial using the installed r7 engine is supported by the reviewed local evidence yet.** The next materially different physical direction is role-specific surface protection and its persistence, but the required measurements and faithful persistent-rock consumer are missing. ## Surface protection: supported process, unsupported intervention

The active [rock representation contract](../../../schemas/rock-fragment-representation-contract.md#surface-support-and-overlap) and reconciled [resumption handoff](resumption-r7-20261103.md) already distinguish surface area, exposed rock, profile mass/volume, residue and overlap. The RUSLE unit repair and surface-support reporting are completed work; they must not be proposed again as Roads remedies.

I checked the actual current-project upload at `/wc1/runs/ro/roads-parameterization-program-20261103/current-project-v2/baseline/roads/roads.uploaded.geojson`. Its SHA256 `8c25c4938f8142395df6be7703bc0ddf2a8ff3f28bf7cb3b2ffa71b5f4385405` matches the reviewed Roads declaration. All 55 features have only **DESIGN, Length, OSUNUM, SURFACE and TRAFFIC** properties. SURFACE is Dirt/Gravel and TRAFFIC is low. Geometry and these categories do not measure armor fraction, particle-size support, litter/rock overlap, compaction, event-day cover or maintenance persistence. The unrutted controller check's 30 m/10% fill and buffer attributes were explicitly supplied by its validation helper; they are not measurements in this upload.

The fresh seven-development-material acquisition binds component, chorizon, chfrags, chtexturegrp and corestrictions tables. Its actual component fields describe survey composition and hydrologic/taxonomic context; horizon fields include texture, density, sieve fragments and retention. `chfrags` contains `chkey, fragvol_r`. None supplies measured engineered road/fill/buffer exposed surface cover or temporal persistence. There is no `cosurffrags` family in this acquisition. Even an additional survey surface-fragment query would remain a component proxy, not a measured Roads armor map. The current project has no surface/rock/cover proxy product in the inspected soils/roads/RUSLE directories. This absence is scoped to the inspected artifacts, not a claim that no field dataset exists elsewhere.

The selected management is also insufficient as physical evidence. B2–B5 use the same bound cropland-format file. Initial canopy/interrill/rill fractions are road 0/0/0, fill 0.2/0.5/0.5 and forest 0.8/1/1. The [input audit](rock-cover-audit-r4/input-findings.md) does not identify which portion represents rock versus litter or vegetation. Equal initial values do not establish equal event-day cover. In particular, an initial forest cover of one does not justify adding another rock-cover fraction.

Readback of exact source `e0abe0581f77c6f66917e90daf8f8f429e9315c8` confirms the missing consumer distinction:

- `infile.for:1465–1486` assigns explicit rock-cover quantities in native land-use 2. These retained Roads decks use native land-use 1.
- `frcfac.for:151–246` uses cropland generic cover for friction; the rock-specific terms at lines 267/304 are in land-use 2. `soil.for:971` uses generic interrill cover for detachment adjustment.
- `covcal.for:147–176` derives cover from residue mass and stubble; `decomp.for:1052` updates it during the history. It is not a persistent inert-rock compartment.
- `scon.for:741–747` also uses profile RFG in consolidation/erodibility adjustment. Changing RFG would alter profile water and another erosion pathway, not isolate surface protection.

Thus profile RFG substitution, a residue addition, a land-use switch, or transferring a RUSLE scalar is not an executable single-mechanism surface-rock test. A measured residue/vegetation history could use its existing native pathway if its mass, species, timing and overlap are supported; the inspected records contain no such history either.

## Concrete evidence acquisition decision

Prioritize identifying an existing **role-tagged surface dataset**, or commissioning that measurement if none exists, before selecting a surface intervention. The needed deliverable is specific:

1. Associate observations with actual road tread, fill and buffer footprints, design, material, traffic and maintenance date. The synthetic climate/material benchmark combinations are not themselves surveyed sites; do not label a MUKEY or climate name a measured road.
2. Retain total-area rock, exposed rock, litter/residue, vegetation and bare-ground observations with co-located overlap information; define particle-size threshold, wood/lithology treatment, units, date, spatial support and uncertainty. Missing remains unknown, distinct from measured zero. Include passing and opposite-direction physical contexts, rather than selecting only surfaces expected to reduce B2/B3 export.
3. Establish whether the legacy generic cover/management assignments were intended to include permanent armor, using original documented field/template provenance. A template value or FSWEPP simulated export is not an independent surface measurement.
4. For vegetation/residue, supply the native-consumed mass/species/maintenance history. For permanent rock, define a persistent quantity with explicit protection/friction/infiltration and overlap behavior, without biomass creation, decomposition substitution or double counting. A new native law still requires scientific support and the existing contract/ADR process; measurements alone do not supply that law.

The concrete external decision is whether a data owner can provide an existing qualifying dataset, or whether a field-measurement program is warranted. No fixed cover value, coefficient range, sampling budget or fitted target is inferable from the current upload. Merely fetching additional profile fragments or repeating the seven-material retention acquisition will not close this gap. Inventorying available datasets and checking their metadata remain routine authorized work; contacting data owners or commissioning measurements was not performed by this assessment.

## Separate supported question: frost policy compatibility

Do not omit this alternative when describing what remains possible. The [canonical frost policy section](../../../schemas/whole-soil-water-capacity-contract.md#frost-policy-and-legacy-compatibility), [lineage investigation](../../20261101_rock_water_development/artifacts/frost-lineage.md) and [F04 readback](../../20261101_rock_water_development/artifacts/F04-science-readback.md) establish a legacy/modern activation difference. A same-source private frost-only intervention activated cold KSFLAG-0 fixtures while preserving warm/already-active controls. In that earlier Roads fixture, annual runoff and sediment remained equal at printed precision despite changes in ordinary outputs. It is not evidence of recovery of the present B2/B3 failures.

Current r7 `winter.for:335–340` still conditions `frostN` on KSFLAG=1 after weather/state eligibility. `infile.for:1608` reading `frost.txt` does not remove that gate. KSFLAG also controls cropland conductivity adjustments (`infpar.for:417,451,475`) and may be reassigned during initialization; changing KSFLAG is not a frost-only treatment. The [final-source legacy renewal](../../20261101_rock_water_development/artifacts/legacy-frost-final-renewal-science-outcome-v2.md) explicitly retains suppressed frost in the KSFLAG-0 Roads fixture; it did not adopt global restoration.

A scientifically distinct native-policy research lane therefore remains conceivable, but no faithful isolated input intervention is available in installed r7. Its missing basis is explicit policy scope plus source-clock fine/coarse liquid/ice and energy/temperature evidence through relevant transitions, with unchanged warm/already-active controls and actual Roads cold exposure. Historical calibration assumptions also remain unresolved. Later snow/restoration numerical repairs are not field validation of that policy. Reusing the old experimental binary, adding `frost.txt`, changing land use, or relabeling KSFLAG sensitivity would not answer the isolated question on r7. No source/build change is proposed or executed here, and no new numerical gate or waiver is invented.
