# Independent scientific review of O3 observations

The complete nine-pair observation dataset is accepted for bounded process
interpretation. No new parameterization, universal correction or native defect
is established by this review. This is independent AI scientific review, not
institutional peer review. O1 and the incomplete O2 attempt remain excluded.

## Evidence and reconciliation

The frozen independent reader passed all 18 full-period lanes: 126 original and
observed ordinary-output pairs rehashed exactly, with 133,468 trace rows and
108 day/OFE groups. Inputs, source records, execution dependencies, logs and
strict particle receipts were checked. Independent implementation review also
reran the strict particle audit over 1,677 EVENT records with zero flags.

The separate [reconciliation review](buffer-process-observation-r4-o3-independent-reconciliation.json)
recomputed all 90 available ELEMENT mass comparisons using the original
±0.0005 kg/m printing bound and all 108 WAT runoff comparisons using each actual
token's printing interval and the native REAL expression. Eighteen absent
ELEMENT rows remain absent. This verifies the observed output boundaries;
it is not an independent whole-profile water-balance proof.

The independent [publication readback](buffer-process-observation-r4-o3-independent-publication-readback.json)
also reproduces all 2,592 hourly REAL return accumulations and 21 observed
ROCHEK-to-IRS runoff additions exactly. It rehashes the frozen 15-file
interpretation packet. The final nine-pair figure is legible, states its mixed
linear/logarithmic scale, and labels selected-day outputs and the represented
first-two-layer storage calculation. Its narrative preserves the causal limits
and controls described below.

All 76 consumed-storage frames reproduce the declared first-two-layer,
connected-length calculation. The largest native-versus-double reconstruction
residual is 1.06×10⁻⁸ m. One independent double summation order differs by
1.39×10⁻¹⁷ m; that failed exact-double review assertion and its correction are
retained separately. The original 3,981-file observation archive excludes those
two later review-attempt files; their
[supplemental archive](revision4-buffer-process-review-failure-retention.json)
provides explicit coverage. No native value or acceptance tolerance changed.
Observed incoming, routed and outgoing class fractions, and observed enrichment
loads, are nonnegative in this bounded panel. This is not full-period internal
class coverage.

## What the observations establish

The legacy target buffers retain 100 mm roughness and 11.45 mm depression
storage. Six generated target buffers have 6 mm roughness and zero depression
storage. Five of those still enter the consolidation/roughness-decay branch;
the meaningful-overprediction case is already consolidated but retains its
previously reduced roughness. Current density equality therefore does not
imply an unchanged 100 mm roughness history. The three generated underprediction
controls retain 100 mm roughness and 11.45 mm depression storage, matching their
legacy counterparts on these quantities.

The first two Denver contrasts demonstrate different retention without increased
reported incoming sediment. On the selected paved day, legacy buffer inflow is
about 198.07 kg and outlet export is zero; generated inflow and outlet are both
about 54.18 kg. The gravel contrast similarly changes from about 172.72 kg
incoming and zero export to about 94.53 kg incoming and exported. Both legacy
dry outlets still execute sediment routing; their retained load is directly
observed, not inferred from missing traces. Both soil lanes have no produced
GRNA excess at the current buffer on these days, while the generated lanes
produce hourly surface return. The immediate difference cannot simply be
called a roughness-triggered GRNA event. Antecedent conditioning and hourly
water allocation remain part of any causal test.

Other targets include positive local **net** exchange. The meaningful passing
control has generated buffer grid net exchange of about +3.111 kg/m versus
zero in its legacy lane, despite lower generated incoming event mass. The
Flagstaff lower-runon contrast changes from about −3.350 kg/m legacy net
exchange to +0.233 kg/m generated. The small passing control also has positive
generated net exchange. These are resolved boundary/grid quantities, not
separate gross erosion/deposition or tagged particle origins.

The opposite direction remains essential. The rutted underprediction control
has equal roughness and depression storage in both lanes, but represented
connected upper storage is about 58.34 mm legacy versus 92.94 mm generated.
Its generated lane has much less incoming sediment and substantial negative
buffer net exchange. The two other underprediction controls also change flow
support and local exchange without changing roughness. A universal roughness
or retention adjustment cannot explain this panel.

The stratum named `underprediction_not_less_supply` has no resolved lower
incoming mass in the printed ledger; it does not have exactly equal native
inflows. The observed tiny incoming loads are approximately 3.38×10⁻⁶ and
2.05×10⁻⁶ kg/m, both printed as zero. Retain this precision distinction.

Applied shear must be evaluated on actual segments. For example, the small
passing generated case has endpoint stress below critical shear but larger
interior normalized shear and an active erosion branch. Endpoint stress alone
would give the wrong explanation. Likewise, the meaningful-overprediction
generated case has greater sediment export despite a lower published peak
than its legacy counterpart. Neither peak flow nor one scalar shear value is
a universal sediment predictor.

## Causal boundary and next study

The native incoming-rate/current-duration representation and upstream event
mass agree at native rounding scale in these observations; no new lost-transfer
defect is demonstrated. Preserve their separate supports rather than assuming
identity by definition. Local grid signs cannot resolve simultaneous subgrid
erosion and deposition. Layer temperature and final routed hydrographs remain
unavailable; frozen state and generated-excess grids do not replace them.

The next controlled study may isolate buffer roughness conditioning while
holding the soil profile, other input parameters, density, engine and full
forcing period fixed. Roughness also affects derived shear/erosion corrections;
allow those operands to respond naturally and observe them. This would be a
roughness-conditioning test, not a depression-storage-only intervention.
It must retain both discrepancy directions and passing controls,
and distinguish event response from antecedent-state changes. A roughness-only
counterfactual can test whether the observed conditioning difference matters;
it cannot by itself establish the physically correct roughness or resolve the
controls with equal roughness and different consumed storage. Do not change
density merely to force a roughness branch, since that would also alter other
soil properties.

The full 116-case context still constrains interpretation: 13 of 18 meaningful
passing controls share the annual lower-fill/higher-outlet pattern, while all
48 meaningful underpredictions have lower generated terminal export. The
selected extremes establish neither prevalence nor an annual causal share.
Any subsequent parameter candidate needs its own physical justification and
broader validation. No ablation was executed by this review.
