# Revision-4 residual structure before the combined experiment

The 96 original magnitude misses remain: 48 meaningful-reference underpredictions,
5 meaningful-reference overpredictions and 43 small-reference exceedances. Every
failed case's native output bytes were rehashed against its accepted U12 record;
the retained audit supplies annual road/fill/buffer boundary exchange.

All 43 small-reference exceedances and all five meaningful overpredictions use
outsloped, unrutted routing. The underpredictions span all four designs (23
outsloped unrutted, 10 vegetated ditch, 8 outsloped rutted, 7 bare ditch). A
single uniform reduction cannot resolve both directions.

The largest small-reference example is Denver material 1718494, paved, low/high
traffic: reference zero, generated export 19.9146 kg/year. Road export is zero;
fill adds 90.5237 kg/year and the buffer has net deposition of 70.6091 kg/year.
Several gravel Flagstaff cases instead carry substantial road export into the
fill, followed by net buffer deposition. These are net boundary exchanges,
not tagged particle-source attribution. They justify retaining all three factor
roles and case-level continuous contrasts; they do not establish a new parameter
value or justify relaxing the small-load criterion.
