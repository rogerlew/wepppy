# Scientific review: whole-hillslope roughness response

The fresh 36-run dataset passes independent readback and the predeclared
selected-panel improvement criterion. This is accepted model-sensitivity
evidence, not an accepted parameterization or physical validation. The review
is independent AI review, not institutional peer review.

## Result and limits

All 18 off histories reproduce the original outputs; all 12 predicted null
histories remain exact. Protected first-two-layer density/porosity and recorded
drivers match in all 18 pairs. All six active generated-soil histories exercise
nonidentity buffer restoration. Full calendars, strict particle audits,
canonical physical checks and source/input/output provenance pass. The copied
verifier preserves original bytes, sizes, modification times and inventories;
176 regenerated tables have identical values, schemas and metadata despite
serialized-byte differences. These accounting gates do not assert complete
physical water closure.

B8 recovers the original annual envelope: export falls from 378.852 to
96.728 kg/year against a 37.286 kg/year reference. Its original export was only
slightly above the ten-times-reference upper boundary. No originally passing
selected generated case regresses. All six active generated histories reduce
annual export, but the three primary failing contrasts remain outside the
envelope:

| Contrast | Off, kg/year | On, kg/year | Original upper bound, kg/year |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| B1 | 7.15547 | 3.83637 | 1 |
| B2 | 18.19041 | 13.87988 | 5.60368 |
| B3 | 8.14689 | 7.19857 | 1 |

The three underprediction controls are unchanged because they already retain
the configured roughness. The persistence rule is therefore insufficient to
resolve the selected discrepancy set. A successful selected-panel criterion
must not be described as general Roads magnitude acceptance. The nine pairs
were deliberately selected contrasts, not a representative independent sample.
Legacy R4 responses remain comparators; they do not replace the original
FSWEPP2/WEPP2010 reference or establish physical truth.

## Process interpretation

At the original target days, all six active generated buffer lanes change their
native interrill delivery factor and delivery fraction from approximately one
to zero. This is a directly observed pathway, not a quantitative decomposition
of annual reduction. Native hydraulic feedback, depression storage, friction,
critical-shear adjustment and endogenous history remain active. No independent
storage-only effect has been measured.

The roughness contribution to critical-shear adjustment increases, but the
final adjustment is already capped in B2, B3 and B8 at those target days.
All selected buffer target-day crust observations report the inactive branch
with factor one; they do not support a target-day crusting explanation. These
bounded observations cannot establish that a pathway is inactive throughout
the full period. Net role-boundary exchange is not gross production/deposition
or tagged particle provenance.

The earlier localization established executed native connected-duration and
persistent-width feedback. This study permits that whole-hillslope response;
it does not validate the physical accuracy of the shared-duration routing law.
Configured 100 mm roughness and suppression of interrill delivery still require
physical/management justification before any default change.

## Disposition

Retain the frozen persistence rule as a research candidate, without fitting a
new value. A broader, predeclared development-panel check of that same rule is
reasonable if pursued; it must preserve the original magnitude and physical
gates and passing/opposite-direction controls. This result does not authorize
parameter promotion, withheld validation, a production change or a UI default.

The original isolated-buffer campaign remains failed. Whole-hillslope attempt
u1 retains four valid histories and stopped for verification-mutation risk;
that stop is not a model or scientific failure. Attempt u2 supplies all 36 fresh
accepted histories. No previous result was substituted into its acceptance.
