## Initial design verdict

The production-template profile panel and public-controller 2 by 2 geometry lane
are appropriate bounded first studies. They measure existing interpretable inputs,
preserve canonical builders and distinguish modeled-area changes from erosion
response. They do not silently adopt conditioned generated soils. Execution approval
is pending concrete runner/declaration inspection; design review alone does not
certify execution or the eventual user workflow.

The four profile contexts include historically high and low export, a canonical
physical-check reference and unrutted role/geometry transformation. Width, fill
length/slope and traffic contrasts plus the width/fill corner can distinguish
geometry scaling, sediment supply and downstream response. Twelve preparation
probes are economical for inactive fill and traffic input aliases. The four full
project runs add saved-state, canonical preparation, native/report and source/baseline
preservation evidence. No full840 repetition is justified.
