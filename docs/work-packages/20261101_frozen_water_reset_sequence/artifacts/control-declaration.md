# Twelve full-routine reset controls

This is a finite mechanism panel, not a hydrology emulator or a proposed model
correction. Link the complete unchanged FRWATC and its twelve source-bound
includes from the prior synthetic build. The authored driver initializes every
observed active field and checks inactive coarse/fine support for mutation.
All driver locals use an rs_ prefix, checked against native include identifiers.
No model assignment or threshold is changed. No model invocation precedes exact
scientific and implementation approval of control-freeze.json.

The exact twelve cases are reset-cases.json, generated by declare_cases.py.
U/P/F zero cases prescribe ST0 against the initial fine-derived YST reference;
the dry case is a no-change control. Signed reference mismatches are deliberate
interface diagnostics, not claims of feasible historical hydrology. The zero
versus 2^-25 positive-ST pair holds a wet fine grid and YST0: the positive value
is below the native absolute gate, while the exact-zero branch directly resets
fine liquid. The thin-support case observes that the exact-zero branch resets
all cells even when positive unfrozen support is below the usual support gate.

Two three-layer controls force a shortage in the first layer. A zero-ST middle
layer resets itself without consuming that shortage; a matched positive middle
layer can consume it. Compare downstream states directly. No absence of a final
carry variable in driver output may be represented as an observed zero carry.

The final case executes direction0, then an explicitly prescribed ST0 interface,
then two direction1 calls without caller advancement of YST. Require the second
reset to be state-idempotent. Its nominal residual can remain nonzero because the
reference was not advanced: it is not a second water loss and not a recreation
of the actual daily caller. Direction0 clearing must be visible before reset.

Use the same standalone compiler flags as the previous checked synthetic panel:
-O0, -fcheck=all, -finit-real=snan, -ffpe-trap=invalid,zero,overflow and
-ffixed-line-length-none. Pin PATH=/usr/bin:/bin and FC=/usr/bin/gfortran, compiler
subtools and runtime hashes. Exactly twelve fresh model processes, 60 seconds
each; preserve every process/build failure and input/output. No adaptive cases.
Fresh output root controls-v1 must not exist. Bind all source, driver, case,
reader, runner and declaration identities before compilation; verify afterward.

The reader requires finite complete stages/layers/fine support, zero inactive
mutation count, exact SLSW=THETDR and NWFRZZ=0 for zero resets, unchanged coarse
fields/ice/geometry and algebraic decomposition. Reconstruction bounds use a
conservative double gamma factor and sums of absolute primitive terms; they do
not turn physical mismatch into conservation acceptance. Paired tests require
subthreshold-positive fine-state preservation, exact-zero wet-fine reset, bottom
layer depletion through a zero middle layer, and unchanged bottom fine state
when the positive middle layer consumes the shortage. All outcomes remain
bounded to these declared inputs. Production and existing history artifacts
are untouched; publish no legacy source or executable.
