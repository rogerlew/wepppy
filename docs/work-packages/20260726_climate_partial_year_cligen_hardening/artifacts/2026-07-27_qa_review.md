# QA Review and Findings Disposition

## Review Scope

An independent QA reviewer evaluated maintainability, failure-mode fidelity,
and whether tests exercise the production call paths rather than only helpers.

## Findings

| Severity | Finding | Disposition |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| High | Tests did not prove different finite cutoffs for radiation, dewpoint, and wind. | Fixed with independent-boundary assertions in full-year Daymet and GridMET CLI tests. |
| High | Initial tests disconnected interpolation missingness from PRN output. | Fixed with producer-level parquet/PRN tests, including `9999` suffix output. |
| Medium | Atomic staging failure cleanup and retry behavior were untested. | Fixed with retained-old-destination, temporary cleanup, and retry-success coverage. |
| Medium | Operator/developer documentation did not describe the new behavior. | Fixed in the climate and CLIGEN READMEs and ADR-0026. |
| High | GridMET finite radiation replacement was not asserted explicitly. | Fixed with a finite-prefix radiation assertion. |
| High | Caller-level primary-variable internal-hole behavior was not proven. | Fixed for the active Daymet and GridMET interpolation producers. |

## Final Disposition

All QA findings were accepted and resolved in this package. The closing
re-review approved the active paths with no remaining QA, maintainability, or
test-quality findings.
