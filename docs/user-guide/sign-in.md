# Signing in to WEPPcloud

## Google, GitHub, or email and password

Use the same email address to access the same WEPPcloud account and projects.

To create an email-and-password account, enter your name and email, then open the verification email to choose your password. If you started with Google or GitHub, open **User Profile** and select **Send password setup email** to add a password.

Existing email-and-password accounts can still sign in. To check your email verification status, open **User Profile**. If verification has not been recorded, select **Send verification email**, open the email, and confirm it. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive; wait a minute before asking for another.

Verifying your email does not change your existing password. If you did not choose that password, use **Reset password** to secure local sign-in. Setting or resetting a password signs out older sessions.

## CILogon — university or organization sign-in

CILogon uses a separate WEPPcloud account, even if your email address is the same. Projects from your other account won’t appear here.

Use CILogon again to return to those projects. Verifying the contact email in that profile does not connect your other accounts or add a password. If CILogon supplies no email address, there is no email available to verify.

## Finding your projects

If your projects are missing, sign out and use the sign-in option you used when creating them. CILogon and your Google/GitHub/email-and-password account remain separate. WEPPcloud will not let you disconnect your last usable sign-in method.
