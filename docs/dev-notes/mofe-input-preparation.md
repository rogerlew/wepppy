# MOFE input preparation and recovery

The [MOFE identity contract](../schemas/mofe-input-identity-contract.md) governs
generated slope, soil, management, cover and PMET inputs. Local undeployed
`wepp_261029` revision 6 repairs per-layer/per-OFE FC/HK ownership and verifies
its existing PMET capacities (32 hill, 15000 watershed) and mapped crop selection.
See the [native coefficient contract](../schemas/native-mofe-coefficient-contract.md).
The older audit checkout has different PMET code; its six-slot limit is not a
universal producer limit. Selected 261029 lacks specialized 9005/revegetation and
RAP cover readers, whose native acceptance remains separate and on hold.

## Users and operators

Preparation preserves each element's soil and complete management calendar. PMET
uses every crop declared by the staged management files. Disturbed coefficients
come from each final element's landuse class and staged surface-soil texture;
existing lookup values and fallback coefficients are unchanged.

Generated crop aliases are deterministic native-safe identifiers, at most eight
characters. The original crop names and selected coefficients are retained in
management header comments. Preserve those comments during export, archive and
restore; they let retries and coefficient mappings continue to use original names.
Provenance follows prepared plant scenarios through subsequent MOFE/rotation
synthesis, including mixed prepared and ordinary sources. A missing alias identity
header is an explicit rebuild error. Ordinary safe-name
controls that need no aliases retain their management bytes.

A failed preparation is not a usable deck. Review the job error and mismatched
slope/soil/management counts, then rebuild through the normal preparation path.
Do not repair counts by deleting elements. PMET publication can leave earlier
files updated when a later replacement fails; visible `*.mipa-*.tmp` files retain
the attempted generated content. Preserve that evidence and rerun preparation.
Generated destinations must be regular files; symlinks are rejected, and replacing
a hardlinked destination preserves the source inode's bytes. No existing projects
are automatically regenerated by this change.

## Developers

`ManagementMultipleOfeSynth.build`, `render` and `write` accept optional
`sim_years=N`. An explicit positive N up to 10000 expands source timelines directly
to exactly N years. Without N, synthesis preserves the least common multiple of
complete source cycles, rejecting periods above 10000 before allocation. Landuse
synthesis has no simulation horizon and intentionally retains the full reusable
period; final WEPP staging expands to the climate's requested horizon.

Multi-OFE stack members must themselves contain exactly one OFE. Preparation
checks actual staged slope, soil and management counts. Residue operations 10/12
retain their source plants. RAP cover writers require contiguous numeric element
IDs and the same element set across all bands/years. Soil version 9005 texture and
developed-landuse restrictive-layer exemptions are evaluated per element.

The paired wepppyo3 source validates finite positive enabled-buffer lengths,
strictly increasing map fractions and positive serialized slope lengths. Equal or
overlong buffers retain the existing single-segment outcome. A tiny segment that
would serialize to zero fails before opening the destination; it is never merged
silently. The installed release extension is not replaced by source validation.

Run management, soils, Disturbed PMET, RAP, staging and archive regression suites;
use actual generated files and reparse them. Native selected coefficients, native
water operands and fresh output/report readback require separate acceptance.

## Revision 6 regeneration

Existing saved outputs do not change. On an authorized rerun, regenerate every
contributing hillslope PASS and watershed output with the matched revision 6 pair.
Inspect per-OFE runoff, storage, ET and drainage: correct ownership can change
heterogeneous histories materially. Do not treat the ownership correction as
new retention defaults, thermal calibration or universal physical validation.
No deployment or bulk regeneration accompanies the local release revision.

## Specialized 9005 and RAP preparation

The [specialized consumer contract](../schemas/specialized-revegetation-consumer-contract.md)
is authoritative for the seven-field soil header, six cover bands, Gregorian
fire date and reference-preserving dynamic behavior. Full `Wepp.prep_hillslopes`
is required: private preparation helpers omit enabled companion files. Cover
years must exactly match climate years; hydrated string year keys are normalized
without changing saved data. Ordinary cover clears stale `simfire.txt`; leaving
9005 clears specialized presence flags and dates. Saved fire-date text is retained,
while emitted `firedate.txt` uses month, day and four-digit year.

Changed prior flags, dates and cover files are retained in visible
`revegetation-input-*` directories under `wepp/runs`; failed attempts remain visible
with `.tmp` names. Archive/restore includes this evidence. Symlink destinations
fail preparation, hardlinked files are replaced without truncating their source,
and existing permissions are preserved. A failed preparation must be resolved
before execution. No existing project is automatically regenerated or switched
to a new executable. Reference fire/canopy/root quirks are deliberate compatibility
exceptions; see ADR-0134 rather than inferring a scientific correction.

For read/dynamic diagnostics, run one isolated hillslope deck at a time with
`wepp_observe.on`. `wepp_rap_read.csv` records stored native bands and dates;
`wepp_rap_dynamic.csv` records soil binding, hydraulic feedback and growth states.
These shared run-directory trace names do not identify concurrent processes;
use separate directories for concurrent observed probes. Keep normal project
execution unobserved and compare ordinary output hashes against isolated probes.

Revegetation preparation also enables the established `.grph.dat` output. Its
legacy `F10.5` disturbance-age field prints asterisks once the age reaches 10,000
days; this writer is unchanged from the bound reference and revision 6. Do not
treat that field as recoverable numeric data. Water interchange and water-balance
reports use `.wat` outputs instead. Dense RAP tracing on NFS can exceed diagnostic
timeouts; retain failed attempts and use isolated local-disk probes for read-state
checks, comparing their ordinary output hashes to the complete normal workflow.
